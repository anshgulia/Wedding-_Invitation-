Destination

Nestled along 40 acres of tropical gardens on Nusa Dua's white-sand coastline, Grand Hyatt Bali is the perfect backdrop for our celebration — minutes from the airport, surrounded by luxury resorts, spas and some of Bali's best beaches.

25–30 minutes from Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS). Airport transfers can be arranged on request.
