Our Story

One Melbourne meeting, several questionable jokes and a very good decision later.

Melbourne  ·  2019

It began the way all great love stories do: two people met in Melbourne and neither realised they had just met the person who would eventually steal their food, blankets and entire future.

Ansh arrived armed with confidence, conversation and what he believed was excellent humour. Shefali arrived with the far more important qualities: good judgement, patience and the ability to laugh at him just enough to keep him encouraged.

What started as an ordinary meeting slowly became longer conversations, spontaneous plans and the comfortable feeling that neither of them needed to pretend to be cooler than they actually were. Melbourne supplied the unpredictable weather; they supplied the chemistry.

Between shared meals, private jokes, adventures and the occasional debate over who was right, they built something that felt less like a dramatic movie romance and more like the best kind of partnership: genuine, ridiculous and entirely their own.

Now the story that began in Melbourne continues beneath the Bali sky, surrounded by the people who helped make every chapter possible.

Plot twist: she said yes.
